Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

DuaLock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores — on your device only

DuaLock keeps the following information locally on your iPhone, solely so the app can work for you:

  • Your onboarding answers (such as your name, age range, and goals), used only to personalize your daily dua plan.
  • Your favorites, playlists, reading history, recitation counters, and streaks.
  • Your app settings, such as language preference and reminder times.

This information never leaves your device. DuaLock has no user accounts, no server, and makes no network connections. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Screen Time & Focus Mode

DuaLock's Focus Mode uses Apple's Family Controls and Screen Time technology to let you block apps you choose until you've engaged with a dua. Which apps you select, and any related usage information, are handled by iOS on your device. DuaLock cannot read, transmit, or share this information, and it is never used for advertising or any other purpose.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Settings.

Children

DuaLock does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children — because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If a future version of DuaLock ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated here first, and the app's App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
