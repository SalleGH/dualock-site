DuaLock Premium

DuaLock requires a DuaLock Premium subscription, offered as a yearly or a monthly auto-renewable subscription. It pays for DuaLock's own content and services: 223 authentic duas from Hisn al-Muslim with their sources, English audio for every dua, your personal Dua Path and reminders, and ongoing updates. Current prices are shown in the app and on the App Store, in your local currency, before you subscribe.

Free trial

New subscribers may be offered a 7-day free trial. DuaLock shows a trial only when your Apple Account is eligible for one. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your subscription starts and Apple charges the subscription price. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Billing and renewal

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when your free trial ends, or at confirmation of purchase if there is no trial.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price and period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

Managing and cancelling

Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or from Manage subscription in DuaLock's Settings. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period. When your subscription ends, Focus Mode turns off and all your apps unlock.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
