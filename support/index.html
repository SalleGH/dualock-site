Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To view, change, or cancel: open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → DuaLock. Cancelling during the free trial means you won't be charged.

Focus Mode & Screen Time permission

Focus Mode needs Apple's Screen Time permission to block the apps you choose. You can grant or revoke it anytime in Settings → Screen Time. DuaLock never sees or shares your app usage — see our Privacy Policy.
