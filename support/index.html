Manage or cancel your subscription

DuaLock Premium is billed by Apple. To change or cancel it:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap DuaLock, then choose a plan or Cancel Subscription.

You can also tap Manage subscription in DuaLock's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore your subscription

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, sign in with the Apple Account you subscribed with, then tap I already have DuaLock Premium on the welcome screen — or Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in DuaLock's Settings.

Redeem a code

In DuaLock, tap Redeem code on the subscription screen or in Settings. Or open the App Store app, tap your profile picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

Focus Mode & Screen Time

Focus Mode uses Apple's Screen Time permission to place a dua before the apps you choose. You pick the apps; DuaLock never sees which ones. Turn Focus Mode off anytime in DuaLock's Focus tab, and manage Screen Time access in iPhone Settings → Screen Time.

If "Your dua is ready" doesn't appear after you tap Read my dua, allow notifications for DuaLock in Settings → Notifications, or simply open DuaLock. If your subscription ends, Focus Mode turns off and every app unlocks automatically.
